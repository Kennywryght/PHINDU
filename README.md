# PHINDU 🏪

**A business tracking & management system for small shops and businesses in Malawi.**

PHINDU helps shop and businesses owners keep accurate records of everything that happens in their business — sales, stock, expenses, customer credit, and daily cash flow — so they always know where their money is and what their business is really doing.

> *"Phindu"* means *"profit"* in Chichewa.

---

## 📖 What It Does

Small shops in Malawi often run on memory and paper. PHINDU replaces that with:

- **Point of Sale** — fast checkout that works for both piece-goods and bulk (kg) products
- **Inventory Management** — track stock levels, batches, price history, and low-stock alerts
- **Sales Records** — every sale, void, and payment tracked with full audit trail
- **Expense Tracking** — record every business expense, grouped by category
- **Customer Credit** — track who owes you what, with full payment history
- **Daily Shift Sessions** — open the day with cash count, close with reconciliation
- **Business Insights** — daily revenue trends, top-selling products, profit margins, peak hours
- **Multi-language** — English and Chichewa (planned expansion)
- **Works on any device** — responsive web app, installable as a PWA on mobile

The goal isn't just to sell — it's to **know your numbers**.
